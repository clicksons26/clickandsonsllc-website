Welcome to Click & Sons

Founded in May 2026, Click & Sons is a family-owned poultry farm focused on building a strong breeding program while helping others start and grow successful flocks of their own.

We believe quality begins with healthy birds, careful breeding, and treating every customer with honesty and respect.
